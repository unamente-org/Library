This is living knowledge that belongs to the people who hold it. We describe it with respect, from what its custodians have made public themselves, and never as a blueprint to copy. If you are one of those custodians, you can ask us to add to an entry, correct it or take it down, and we will act on what you tell us. Write to us at [info@unamente.org](mailto:info@unamente.org). The picture above is our own illustration. An entry carries a picture only when its custodians give us one.
