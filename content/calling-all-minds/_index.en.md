These are questions about the solutions in our library that nobody has answered yet, as far as we can find. Each one says what we already know, when and in which languages we searched, and what would settle it. If you farm, build, teach or do research, or are looking for a thesis topic, pick one, test it, and tell us what you find through [the Get Involved page](https://www.unamente.org/get-involved); we check what you send like any other source before it goes into the library.
