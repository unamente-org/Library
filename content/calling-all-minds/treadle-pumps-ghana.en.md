## What we know

Our Treadle Pump entry (trust: Strong) says the pump spread in Bangladesh because a market of makers and dealers grew up around it. In Ghana, a study by the International Water Management Institute, with the country director of the promoter among its authors, found that about a fifth of 52 buyers had stopped using their pump within about two years, with breakdown the reason named most often. The organization promoting the pump, Enterprise Works, left when its project ended in 2004. Sales fell sharply in 2005, and only nine of the 21 makers it had trained were still producing pumps.

## What we could not find

We searched on 22 November 2026, in English. We found no study of Ghana's treadle pumps later than 2005. We could not search in Twi or Ewe.

## What limits it for real

A treadle pump runs on human energy, so it suits small plots near shallow water. Its seals and parts wear out and must be replaced.

## What would settle it

Return to Ashanti and Volta, the regions where the pumps were sold. Find out how many are still in use, who repairs them and where the parts come from, and what the farmers who stopped use now.
